## Trickster Directory

The directory includes eleven independently checked short reference profiles, each with original editorial text and outbound source links. Relevant sources include the Library of Congress, Smithsonian institutions, UNESCO, Te Ara, and other named reference publishers; the full citations appear on the individual profiles and in `src/data/tricksters.json`. These sources support factual descriptions but do not license the use of their imagery or article text.

[Aadip Desai’s Trickster Hit List!](https://www.aadip.com/trickster/) (139 numbered entries, accessed November 2026) served as a **research starting point** only. The site does not mirror that compilation. Potential additional entries are maintained separately as unpublished candidates; they require independent verification and editorial review. No scraped or unlicensed character artwork is included.
